conditionalFields

Shows a group of fields only while another field asks for it — and takes it out of the submit and the validation while it is closed.

Five forms, one component. Each group is a plain <fieldset> with one attribute, data-conditional-when. While it is closed it is hidden and disabled — so the fields in it are not only out of sight, they are out of the submit and out of the validation.

1 — A checkbox, required fields, and formValidation

The delivery fields carry data-conditional-required, which the module turns into required — so without the script they are optional, and the full form can still be sent. Closed, they do not count: send the form with the box unticked and it goes through. The panel below shows what a submit would send.

2 — By value: a select and a radio group

contactBy=phone|sms opens on either value; newsletter!=none opens on anything but “none”. Change something, then press Reset: the groups follow the restored defaults, although a reset fires no input. The select is here to show that it works; in a real form prefer radios, because on Windows the arrow keys change a closed select — and its groups — with every press.

4 — Three mistakes you can read in the console

A condition naming a field this form does not have, and the attribute on a <div>, which cannot disable what it holds. Both groups stay visible — a group shown by mistake costs a glance, one hidden by mistake costs the fields in it. The third group works, but sits above its switch, where a reader who ticks the box will not meet it: that is reported too.

5 — A switch outside the form

The checkbox sits outside the <form> and joins it through form="d5-form" — a layout that puts a field in a sidebar or a page header does that. The group still follows it, and the panel shows it is sent with the form.
