filterList

Filters a card grid that is already in the page — by what you type, by the facets you tick, and out loud.

Four filters, one component. What you see is this page's own CSS reacting to a single attribute — data-filterlist-state — and to hidden. What you cannot see is the half that matters: a polite live region in every one of them, saying in words how many cards are left.

1 — A search field over a card grid

data-filterlist-query on the field, data-filterlist-item on each card, and nothing else. The haystack is the card's own visible text, so “rebuilding” finds the card whose blurb says it — example 2 shows how to narrow that down.

2 — Facets, a reset, and word matching

A <select> and a checkbox group, each control's value matched against the card's data-filterlist-tags: OR inside a group, AND across groups. The cards carry data-filterlist-text, so the search runs on the name, the role and the city and not on the blurb, and data-filterlist-match="word" narrows a term to the start of a word: des finds design, sign no longer does. Try zurich — it finds Zürich.

3 — Cards that arrive later

The button under the grid is this page's own script appending plain markup — no module involved, nothing re-initialised. A MutationObserver re-indexes the region, so filter first and then load: the cards arriving obey the query that is already in the field. Custom wording here too — data-filterlist-count-text for the number you can see.
