CMS-maintainable video embeds: self-hosted with responsive sources and art direction, or YouTube behind a GDPR-friendly click-to-load facade.

Resize the window (or rotate your device) to watch the source switching: the DevTools network panel shows which rendition loads, and in example 3 the footage itself changes shape. Playback position survives every switch. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the ambient loop in example 2 starts paused.

1 — Click to play, resolution ladder

One footage, five renditions from 640 px to 4K: picked by viewport width and pixel density (min-resolution: 2dppx), first match wins, the attribute-less source is the fallback. The centered play button hands over to the native controls; pausing brings it back.

2 — Ambient loop (autoplay, muted)

autoplay muted loop playsinline on the <video>: it runs natively, the script only adds the persistent corner toggle (WCAG 2.2.2). Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it does not start — the toggle is the explicit opt-in.

3 — Art direction (portrait ↔ landscape)

Below 768 px the container is 9:16 and a portrait rendition (c_fill crop of the same footage) loads; from 768 px up both switch to 16:9. Start the video, then resize across the breakpoint: the source swaps and the playback position carries over.

4 — YouTube facade (click to load)

A locally hosted poster and a play button — check the network panel: nothing is requested from Google until you click. The click builds the youtube-nocookie.com iframe with autoplay; without JavaScript the link opens the video on YouTube. The CMS field accepts the full URL or the bare id.
