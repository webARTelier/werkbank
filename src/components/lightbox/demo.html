lightbox

Image and gallery viewer: a list of links to full-size images, opened in place in a native <dialog>.

Gallery

Six images, no loop: open one and step through the set with the side controls or the arrow keys. At the first and last image the control that would lead nowhere is aria-disabled — it keeps its place, its name and its tab stop. The caption under each thumbnail is the same text the viewer shows.

Opened from a separate control

data-lightbox-open: these three controls are not images and are not even inside the gallery — they open the one above, which carries id="lightbox-gallery". The third one adds data-lightbox-index="3" and starts at the waterfall; the index is counted from 1, the way the counter in the viewer counts. An opener inside its own gallery can leave the value empty and needs no id at all.

Single image

The module on the link itself: that one link is the whole set, so the viewer opens without navigation controls and without a counter — only the picture, its caption and the way out.

A figure in the middle of an article is the other half of this component: nothing about it is a gallery, it just wants to be readable at full size.

Looping, and in another language

data-lightbox-loop: stepping past an end wraps around, so neither control is ever disabled. The captions come from data-lightbox-caption on the links instead of from the tiles — and every string of the generated chrome is set in German here, including the counter and the announcement template. The gallery carries lang="de", which the module mirrors onto the injected viewer: it lives on <body>, so it would otherwise be pronounced as English.
